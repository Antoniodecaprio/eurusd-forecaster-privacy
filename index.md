# Privacy Policy — EUR/USD Forecaster

Effective date: [2026-11-7]
Last updated: [2026-11-8]

This Privacy Policy explains how **[Developer / Company Name]** ("we", "us",
"our") handles information in connection with the **EUR/USD Forecaster**
mobile and web application (the "App").

We built the App to require as little of your data as possible. There are
no accounts, no logins, no advertising identifiers, and no sale of data of
any kind.

---

## 1. Summary (plain language)

- We do **not** require you to create an account.
- We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos,
  or location.
- We do **not** sell or rent any data to anyone.
- The App fetches **public financial data** (exchange rates and inflation
  indices) to compute forecasts. That data contains nothing about you.
- Our server may automatically process **technical request data** (such as
  your IP address and a timestamp) solely to operate, secure, and rate-limit
  the service.
- All forecasts are computed on our server and returned to your device. We
  do not store your inputs or results after the response is sent.

## 2. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect, request, or access:

- Your name, email address, postal address, or phone number
- Your precise or approximate location
- Your contacts, calendar, photos, camera, or microphone
- Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID)
- Your browsing history outside the App
- Any biometric data
- Any health, financial-account, or payment information

The App contains no advertising SDKs and no third-party tracking SDKs.

## 3. Information processed when you use the App

### 3.1 Forecast inputs you enter
You may enter parameters such as a date range, forecast horizon, and
inflation assumptions. These are transmitted to our server to compute the
result and are **not retained** after the response is returned. We do not
build a history of your queries.

### 3.2 Technical request data
When your device contacts our server, standard technical information is
automatically transmitted as part of any internet request. This may include:

- IP address
- Date and time of the request
- App version and operating system type
- General error or diagnostic information if a request fails

This information is used **only** to operate the service, prevent abuse
(rate limiting), diagnose failures, and maintain security. It is retained in
server logs for a limited period (typically no more than 30 days) and is not
used for profiling, advertising, or marketing.

### 3.3 Public financial data
The App retrieves publicly available economic series (for example, EUR/USD
reference rates, US CPI, and Eurozone HICP) from public data providers such
as the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED). This data is public
economic data and contains no personal information.
